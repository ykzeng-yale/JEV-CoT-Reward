# V3 development decision

The audited 24-problem replication does not support a benefit of semantic selection in the frozen three-candidate,2048-token design. The release and analysis preserve all tasks and outcomes. No API/local-judge failures occurred; all pools contained three distinct texts. This excludes those particular implementation failures, not weak semantic discrimination, insufficient continuation budget, or noisy candidate values.

Jev and local selection have identical success totals despite not necessarily choosing identical candidates. Their empirical zero-width paired interval is not an equivalence certificate. Independent two-repeat candidate selection/evaluation produced no advantage, but two samples per candidate are too noisy to rule out latent useful heterogeneity.

Decision: do not immediately multiply this fixed-design sample or spend more Jev calls. Finish the predeclared segmented sham/GUARD qualifications and CUDA development comparison. They test whether state-dependent branching has usable benefit and quantify interruption/finalization costs before adding a semantic controller. A future controller study must include a development-frozen rate-matched randomized intervention, a best-static policy, strong cheap features and independent local judging, with identical action/finalization contracts. Freeze that study after complete development audits, not from partial cluster outcomes.

This decision neither closes the broad research program nor promotes a negative result into a general impossibility claim. Preserve the closed v1 test and do not tune on it.
