## Design and evaluation reflection / 设计错误与修正边界

The early program spent too long on judge agreement and small repair/branch screens before establishing downstream action usefulness and a strong cheap comparator. Total output-token caps also failed to equalize prefill, intervention counts, discarded work and timeout exposure. Look-only ambiguity was incorrectly generalized to the full controller-visible input; repeated seeds and overlapping fitted folds were insufficient for independent-policy or population claims. These are material design limitations, not cosmetic reporting issues. They explain why the accumulated compute does not currently support scaling the original Jev claim.

Three source-proven corrections improve *evaluation reliability* without changing outcomes: faithful conductivity replay removes an RNG-consuming extra legality query and restores chronology; the ReCoMA auditor checks numeric/string scalar representations against the same pinned official `score/maxScore`; and it reconstructs the generator's `role`/`content` key order lost by sorted JSON logging. Generic string EM is ignored. The original failed audits and original source remain preserved, and malformed/nonfinite/unequal endpoints or altered message content still fail. [Auditor](../scripts/audit_recoma_discoveryworld_v3.py), [runtime contract](recoma_scientific_failure_contract_v3.md), [analysis contract](recoma_baseline_analysis_contract_v3.md).

These checks remain bounded: the conductivity runner/replay share an endpoint helper, and DiscoveryWorld's progress/success ultimately share the simulator's official scorecard source. Independent implementation/recomputation and source checks reduce error; they are not two independent world-state oracles. Visible-thought text is not hidden CoT. A complete one-arm baseline can describe behavior, failures and costs but cannot identify the causal benefit of a controller or an externally valid treatment effect.

A confidence interval containing zero is **inconclusive**, not a stop proof; a favorable point estimate is not a continue proof. To promote a source-specific efficacy claim, an independent frozen evaluation must show advantage over the training-selected strongest comparator after acquisition/prefill/discarded-work/failure costs. Require a lower uncertainty bound above zero and a point estimate meeting a **predeclared practical margin**; futility needs a justified upper bound below that margin. Cost coefficients must come from an external deployment/resource valuation, not be chosen to make current subgroup effects positive. A 100%-accurate cheap meter may still invite a cost-saving question, but that question has not been tested here.

The baseline census is one-arm description with 24 template/difficulty strata. Under the protocol's optimistic hypothetical paired-binary assumptions (30% discordance, two-sided 0.05, 80% power), 120/48/24 independent units detect approximately 13.87/21.61/29.81 pp; actual clustering can worsen precision. A 3-pp contrast needs approximately 2,614 independent tasks, a 5-pp contrast 940. Neither repeated calls nor parametric seeds create those independent units. This six-hour phase cannot honestly confirm a small Jev increment.
