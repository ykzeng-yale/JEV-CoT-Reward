# JEV-CoT-Reward: independent investment and scientific-validity review

Review date: September 30, 2026. Scope: current manuscript and theory, completed-study analyses/auditors, frozen protocols/source, central Jev cost reconciliation, current Slurm accounting and submission receipts. Three independent reviews covered empirical results, design/evaluation and theory/prior art. The master manuscript was reviewed, not edited. This is a decision assessment, not a declaration that the research program is complete.

## Decision / 投资判断

**Do not increase investment in the original Jev-specific efficacy or general-method claim on the present evidence.** There is no replicated positive Jev increment over strong cheap/local alternatives. The new conductivity result is a clear positive *measurement-information* result, but an executable no-model/no-Jev meter already achieves 150/150. It therefore qualifies a useful action while removing observed accuracy headroom for Jev on this panel.

A limited next phase is defensible only if it resolves a concrete remaining decision: an observable, naturally occurring state where different actions have different cost-adjusted preferred choices, beyond always using the strongest cheap baseline. Do not buy further large experiments merely because an action has a positive mean effect, the theory is correct conditionally, or resources exist. If the objective remains a broadly novel Jev/CoT/reward method, current evidence and prior art do not support that investment. If the objective becomes a rigorous source-comparison or negative/reproducibility study, there is a possible narrower contribution, whose merit remains to be demonstrated.

## Was experiment design/evaluation correct? / 对此前工作的反思

Not throughout. Several genuine errors or limitations must be owned explicitly:

1. **The look-only split conclusion overreached.** It omitted visible task descriptions/legal actions. Identical look strings can have different hidden targets, but this does not prove ambiguity for the actual full-input controller. Full-input disjointness corrects that finding; it does not prove freedom from public-source/seed shortcuts.
2. **The original conductivity replay was not faithful.** Extra queries perturbed RNG; chronological context was incomplete in some diagnoses. Corrected strict full replay establishes the saved panel's validity. The previous premature unauditable-stop proposal was not justified.
3. **Generator-token caps were not total compute matching.** Prefill, discarded candidates, segmentation, local judging, hosted acquisition and per-call timeouts matter.37vs14branches cannot support a matched-rate contrast. Equal padded32actions are not equal intrinsic work.
4. **Early screens were too small and too numerous.** Useful downstream action value should have been qualified before extended judge-centric development. Small favorable directions motivated replication; replication did not support unchanged scaling. These are development results, not a sequence of confirmatory successes.
5. **Cross-fitting was oversold if interpreted as independent evaluation.** Grouping prevents direct same-problem leakage, but overlapping fitted folds remain dependent; fixed-contribution bootstrap ranges are not a general95%coverage certificate. Independent selected-policy evaluation is required; retrospective best-arm choice is optimistic.
6. **Record audits are valuable but have a boundary.** Exact AST/Fraction and graph validation, token/call reconstruction, preserved failures and frozen manifests support principal counts. Most historical checks are not fresh model inference replay, independent weight attestation or proof of causal generalization. No principal completed-study count was overturned in this review.
7. **Our generator produces visible explanations, not inaccessible internal CoT.** Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 is non-thinking. Evidence concerns interventions on generated visible text. No implemented Jev-trained reward/RL improvement is established.

## Theory and innovation / 理论是否有用、是否新

The principal identification, decision-value, greedy regret, frozen-policy concentration, transfer, sequential performance-difference and weighted binary-logistic results are valid under their stated conditions. An independent focused suite passed20tests; finite tests are not proofs. I found no major algebraic contradiction. The empirical conditions remain unverified in important ways:

- Free information has nonnegative value only for an ideal decision maker allowed to ignore it. It need not have strictly positive value, help a finite learner or justify acquisition costs.
- If the same action is optimal everywhere, even a perfectly informative judge has zero action-selection benefit. Buying it can reduce utility. The meter result illustrates the practical need for a *choice* gate.
- Sequential safety needs valid action-value error bounds on all histories reached after intervention; prefix calibration/subjective confidence does not supply them.
- Transfer needs support overlap and bounded value drift; it does not guarantee new tasks/backends/models work.
- Jev transforms supplied history into a feasible representation; it does not automatically provide independent external information.

One minor formal defect remains at `paper/main.tex:110`: “Any classifier” should be “Any deterministic classifier,” or the randomized version should be stated in expectation. With identical observation and labels(0,1), independent fair-coin predictions produce zero realized errors with probability1/4, despite minimum expected errors1. The master file was not changed in this comments-only review.

The broad method is already occupied. Primary sources rechecked:

- [Calibration Is Not Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21399): intervention advantage, same-prefix branching and action-conditioned control.
- [DIAL](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.06908v1): signal-independent exploration and learned computation-allocation decisions.
- [Selecting Computations](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.5879): established metareasoning/value-of-computation basis.
- [Learning What to Skip](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30734): counterfactual omission, action-specific safety models, calibration and domain guards.

Thus the estimand, replay protocol, real-time judging/repair/stop and elementary VOI/policy-improvement arguments are not defensible methods-level novelty. A particular tightly controlled Jev-vs-cheap/local judgment-source comparison could remain an empirical extension, but its positive effect and contribution have not been established. The100%-accurate scripted meter is a useful baseline, not novel scientific-discovery capability.

## What would justify continuing? / 可接受的下一次投入

1. Preserve the qualified meter and negative studies. No repeated tiny meter pilots, unchanged repair/branch scale-up, training grid or confirmatory expansion is justified now.
2. If pursuing DiscoveryWorld, first repair scientific-vs-infrastructure failure accounting and freeze an end-to-end **single-GPU** qualification. Require durable failed episodes, endpoint integrity, parser/error-path tests, pinned runtime, peak memory and measured tokens/actions/latency. Only those measurements can size a full request. This is a proposed prerequisite, not a launched experiment or new result.
3. Before another judge/controller trial, identify a specific natural decision boundary: some observable states should favor different actions under a predeclared cost model, and a strong cheap/static baseline must leave practically relevant headroom. Action mean benefit alone is insufficient.
4. Freeze one primary contrast against strong cheap/executable/local and compatible published controls, training/tuning allowances, all acquisition/prefill/discarded-work/failure costs, practical benefit margin, independent-unit sample size, fresh evaluation and one explicit resource cap. Preserve the closedv1 and untouched final evaluations.
5. A positive controller development estimate only warrants independent confirmation. Continued scale requires uncertainty narrow enough to resolve the practical margin; failure to reject zero is not equivalence. If the bounded gate fails or remains unresolved, stop/pivot the Jev-specific efficacy expansion rather than spend until a favorable subgroup appears.
